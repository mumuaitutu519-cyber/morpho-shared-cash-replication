Daily duplication controls: lagged cash concentration, log total assets and position-weighted utilization per 10 points. Daily J/assets adds lagged Spark committed net flow / aggregate assets. Asset-weighted duplication is sum(assets_v*D_v)/sum(assets_v). Dates with a positive-assets zero-S vault have an undefined weighted outcome and are excluded from that outcome only; these dates are listed in robustness_status.json. Spark exclusion recomputes the nine-vault network and controls. HAC14 columns omit year-month and weekday FE. Other daily columns use HAC7. Cross-chain controls match the frozen cross-chain round; leave-chain-out regressions recompute FE and cluster counts, retaining within-chain D. Market regressions use duplication as outcome, market/date FE and two-way clustered CR1 SE, t reference min(G,T)-1; utilization is per 10 points, cash is log million USDC. Low group has vault count <= median; high group > median. Negative two-way covariance diagonals are reported as missing SE, without replacement. *, **, *** denote 10%, 5%, 1%; parentheses contain t statistics.
