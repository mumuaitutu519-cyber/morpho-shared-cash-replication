Current-state sample: 2141 positive-assets, positive-S vault-states on four chains. Refusal is assessed at the vault next observed positive-assets state, including S=0 at that next state. D and controls are measured at the current state; Flow is committed deposits minus withdrawals from UTC midnight to the current snapshot block, million USDC. The next-observation interval is variable and reported in task3_cross_status.json. Chain-by-state fixed effects, vault-cluster CR1 covariance, t(G-1) for LPM; logit uses clustered normal-reference statistics and McFadden pseudo R-squared. Logit omits constant-outcome FE strata, with counts in the model file. *, **, *** denote 10%, 5%, 1%; parentheses show t or logit z statistics.
