# Follow-up after daily bounds
The daily zero lower bounds motivate checking the observation boundary; this is a post-result sensitivity analysis, not a pre-specified confirmation. Use the entire original January 16–November 30 study period, without choosing a favorable start/end. Retain tagged gross treasury receipts across days; opening cash and all other receipts remain alternative funding. Returned receipts are not permanently tagged. Targets remain borrow and withdrawals outside the Spark vault; other holders withdrawing through that vault are excluded, so this is not an exhaustive other-holder payment measure. A greedy feasible allocation is only a witness, not an optimal lower bound; use a separate sparse LP to establish a lower endpoint. This cannot identify payments that would disappear under behavioral removal of Spark.
