# R29: treasury cash provision feasibility study

Objective: determine whether verified Spark treasury operations replenish common-market cash and have an identifiable accounting contribution to other participants' completed payments. This is not a regression-significance search or a causal removal experiment.

Inputs: full retained common-market principal-event ledger, vault beneficiary-event ledger, and existing zero-gap market cash validation. Window: January 16–November 30, 2025; reconstruct opening cash from inception, not a zero at each day.

1. Strict operation link: a transaction contains only treasury beneficiary deposits or only treasury beneficiary withdrawals, no other beneficiary deposits/withdrawals, and common-market vault supply/withdrawal totals exactly equal those vault flows, with no opposite market flow by the vault. Separate mismatches, mixed directions and mixed beneficiaries. Equality is an operation link, not permanent identity of fungible units.
2. Reconstruct cash at every ordered market event and aggregate all within-transaction market operations. Distinguish the tagged treasury amount from the transaction's net market cash change. Count clean transactions with no other market operation separately. Compare deposits with actual transaction-opening cash; do not call a selected coverage threshold an estimated stress event.
3. Daily first-use funding bounds: opening market cash and all untagged positive receipts are alternative funds; strictly linked treasury supplies are tagged new receipts. All Borrow and Withdraw cash outflows compete. Minimize/maximize tagged contribution to (a) borrowing, (b) withdrawals by market position owners other than the Spark vault, and (c) their union. Returned/repayed funds are alternative receipts; a new verified treasury supply is a new gross funding receipt. Bounds measure gross daily first uses, not net external capital, causal credit creation, or funds' geographic origin. Optimize joint target groups, not marginal endpoint sums. Include all days and preserve zero lower bounds.
4. Summarize cash changes and funding attribution in dollars, transactions and days. Distinguish immediate supply capacity, completed recipient payments and contract-executable holder withdrawal limits. Existing market cash records alone cannot establish a particular peer holder's full contract capacity or unmet demand. If support is material, specify a bounded direct-state follow-up; do not infer it from the mechanical identity cash_after = cash_before + supply.

Validation: exact nonnegative cash recursion; opening/end balances align with the existing historical ledger; identical transaction IDs and log order; complete coverage of linked/unlinked vault operations; sparse inventory LP feasibility and endpoint witnesses, with integer micro-USDC endpoint checks. No data trimming to strengthen results.
