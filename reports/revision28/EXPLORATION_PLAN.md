# R28 bounded exploratory analysis

Written before running the following extension, with R26/R27 results already known. This is exploration, not preregistration or confirmation on unseen data. The purpose is to identify a coherent economic account of treasury deployment and holder heterogeneity, not to iterate specifications until a star appears.

A. Treasury gross deposits, gross withdrawals and net deposits. Same 319 dates and identical R27 covariates (lagged premium/utilization, ETH return/volatility, lagged net deposits, log vault assets, month/weekday). This permits an exact coefficient decomposition: net = deposit - withdrawal. Three additional rate equations divide each outcome by prior treasury claim and multiply by 100; do not trim early or large-flow dates. Six equations, 12 focal tests.
B. Three- and seven-day cumulative treasury net deposits, from t through t+h-1, predicted by t-1 conditions with the same controls and complete horizons. HAC bandwidth max(7,h), bandwidth14 sensitivity. Two equations, four focal tests. These are overlapping cumulative associations, not causal local projections or independent replications of the daily result.
C. Non-Spark incumbent deposit and withdrawal indicators, same >=1 USDC risk set. Owner AND date fixed effects. Interact log opening claim with prior premium and utilization; also size interactions with ETH return and volatility. Main log claim remains; common daily variables are absorbed by date effects. Account age is collinear with owner/date effects and excluded. Two equations, four focal tests. Effects describe differences by observed position size, not exogenous wealth or liquidity treatments. Two-way owner/date covariance with absorbed degrees of freedom, retain constant-outcome owners and exclude singletons.

Store every model, coefficient, covariance and sample. Main extension family = 20 focal tests, with Holm and BH adjustments. Also report a cumulative search family adding the six nonduplicate focal tests from R27 (the original Spark daily two tests coincide with A's dollar net equation): 26 tests. Sensitivities do not reset these families. Coherent result blocks may enter the paper; full search ledger remains accessible. No significance-driven variable dropping, new windows, winsorization, or repeated threshold searches.

Validation: identical-X cash-flow coefficient identity, independent FE transformation check on a deterministic subset, covariance/rank/convergence diagnostics, and leave-one-month-out sign/range stability for the focal treasury utilization relation. That stability check uses seen data and is not out-of-sample confirmation.
